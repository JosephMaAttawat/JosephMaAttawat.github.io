About

I received my PhD in Politics and Public Administration from The University of Hong Kong, following an MA in International Relations from Waseda University and a BA (Honours) in Political Science from Chulalongkorn University.

My research examines how political narratives, identity, reputation, and strategic communication shape public attitudes towards states and international relations, with a regional focus on Thailand, China, and Southeast Asia.
