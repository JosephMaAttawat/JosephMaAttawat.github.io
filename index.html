About

Researching how people understand states, identities, and international relationships

I am a political scientist specialising in political communication, political psychology, and China–Southeast Asia relations. My research examines how identity, narratives, and strategic communication shape public attitudes towards countries and international relations.

I received my PhD in Political Science from The University of Hong Kong, following an MA in International Relations from Waseda University and a BA (Honours) in Political Science from Chulalongkorn University. My doctoral research examined Chinese ethnicity and attitudes towards China through psychological insights from Thailand.
